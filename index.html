Safety First & Emergency Guidelines

Awareness and preparation are your best defenses against landslide hazards. Familiarize yourself with these crucial safety steps to protect your family and community.

Preparation (Before)

  • Identify nearby landslide hazard zones.
  • Develop a family evacuation plan and route.
  • Assemble an emergency go-bag (food, water, first-aid).
  • Listen to local weather forecasts and LIGTAS advisories.

Action (During)

  • Stay alert and awake during heavy, continuous storms.
  • Listen for unusual sounds like trees cracking or boulders knocking.
  • Move away from the path of the landslide quickly.
  • If escape is not possible, curl into a tight ball and protect your head.

Recovery (After)

  • Stay away from the slide area; further danger may exist.
  • Check for injured/trapped persons near the slide without entering it.
  • Listen to local radio or authorities for emergency information.
  • Report broken utility lines to appropriate authorities.

⚠️ Monitoring & 🚨 Alert Levels

Our sensors track ground movement and soil saturation in real-time. Understand what each alert level means for your immediate safety.

Level 1: Advisory

  • Status: Normal to minor rainfall recorded.
  • Action: Continue normal activities. Stay updated with daily weather forecasts.

Level 2: Watch

  • Status: Continuous heavy rainfall. Soil saturation increasing.
  • Action: Prepare emergency kits. Be ready for possible evacuation. Monitor local alerts closely.

Level 3: Warning

  • Status: Critical sensor thresholds breached. Landslide is imminent or occurring.
  • Action: EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY to designated safe zones. Follow instructions from local DRRM officers.

🏥 Emergency Response

Immediate Post-Disaster Protocol

In the event of a confirmed landslide, local emergency responders and medical teams will be deployed. Do not attempt rescue operations in unstable terrain without specialized training. Direct all emergency requests to your local Barangay DRRM Council or call the national emergency hotline.

Evacuation Centers: Ensure you proceed only to pre-approved structural evacuation centers identified during community drills. Temporary tents and medical triage will be set up at these coordinates.
